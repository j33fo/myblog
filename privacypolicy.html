KivvoLog – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Developer and publisher: PuiVerzi, United Kingdom
Contact: tzampu@protonmail.com
App: KivvoLog: Purchases Remembered
Package: com.puiverzi.kivvo

1. Overview

KivvoLog is a local-first purchase and receipt management application. It is designed to help you remember purchases, receipts, return periods and warranty periods.

KivvoLog does not require an account and PuiVerzi does not operate a KivvoLog server for storing your purchase records or receipt images.

2. Information stored locally

Information you enter or create in KivvoLog is stored locally on your device. This can include purchase details, dates, prices, return and warranty information, and receipt images.

Receipt scanning and OCR are performed on-device. Receipt images are not uploaded to PuiVerzi's servers for OCR or storage.

3. Camera and receipt scanning

KivvoLog requests camera permission to allow you to photograph receipts. The app can automatically capture and crop receipts and extract information using on-device OCR. OCR results are presented for your review and correction before you save a purchase.

4. Gallery access and saved receipt images

If you enable the Auto-save to Gallery setting, KivvoLog copies receipt images to a public gallery folder such as Pictures/KivvoLog. Images stored in your public gallery may be accessible to other apps on your device and may be backed up by services such as Google Photos according to your device and cloud-backup settings. PuiVerzi does not receive those images merely because you saved them to your gallery.

5. Notifications and background operation

KivvoLog may request permission to send notifications and use Android alarm functionality to provide return and warranty reminders. The app may run a startup receiver after your device restarts solely to reschedule local reminders. This functionality is not used by PuiVerzi to track your location, activity or behaviour.

6. Biometric app lock

KivvoLog Plus may provide an app-lock feature using Android's biometric authentication system. KivvoLog does not see, receive, or store your fingerprint, face scan, or other biometric information. Authentication is handled by the Android operating system and KivvoLog receives only the authentication result needed to continue or deny access.

7. Advertising

Free-tier versions of KivvoLog may display banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob is a third-party advertising service and may process information such as advertising/device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions and other information necessary to provide, measure, personalise or protect advertising, subject to Google's policies and the settings applicable to your device and region.

PuiVerzi does not receive your purchase records or receipt images through AdMob and does not send those records to AdMob for advertising.

Where required by applicable law, KivvoLog uses an appropriate consent mechanism for personalised advertising and related data processing. You may also have controls through your device or Google's advertising settings.

For information about how Google uses data in apps that use its advertising services, see Google's information about how it uses data from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

8. Analytics and crash reporting

KivvoLog does not currently use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or another third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. PuiVerzi does not independently collect analytics profiles or crash reports from KivvoLog.

Third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob may perform their own measurement and reporting as described in their applicable policies.

9. Data export and import

KivvoLog can export your locally stored purchase data as JSON or CSV and can import supported data back into the app. Exported files are stored wherever you choose to save them. Once exported, their security depends on the location and service you use to store or share them.

10. Data deletion

KivvoLog provides local deletion functionality. Because purchase data is designed to remain on your device rather than on a PuiVerzi server, PuiVerzi does not maintain a remote account database containing your KivvoLog purchase records.

Copies of receipt images saved to your public gallery, exported files, device backups, or cloud storage are outside KivvoLog's direct control and may need to be deleted separately.

11. Information PuiVerzi does not collect

KivvoLog does not require or collect your name, email address, account credentials, payment-card information, purchase history on PuiVerzi servers, receipt images on PuiVerzi servers, or location history.

PuiVerzi does not sell your purchase records or receipt images.

12. Children's privacy

KivvoLog is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. KivvoLog does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a PuiVerzi account because KivvoLog does not require accounts.

13. Third-party services

KivvoLog may rely on third-party services or platform components, including Google AdMob and Android system services. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KivvoLog's functionality or legal requirements change. The updated version will be made available at the published Privacy Policy address.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or KivvoLog's privacy practices, contact PuiVerzi at tzampu@protonmail.com.
